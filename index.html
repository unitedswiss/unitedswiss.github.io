We do not chase markets. We study them, the way glaciologists study ice: patiently, by measurement, across long horizons.

Every investment is
an experiment.

We begin from a hypothesis and then try, deliberately, to break it. A thesis that cannot be falsified is not a thesis. Four variables decide whether capital moves.

C = N·D·A F
  1. N

    Necessity

    Is the product load-bearing? We favour what customers cannot remove without something breaking. Systems, not features.

  2. D

    Durability

    Will the advantage survive a decade of change in technology, regulation and taste? We measure moats in years, not quarters.

  3. A

    Alignment

    Do the incentives of founders, customers, regulators and capital point the same way? Misalignment compounds faster than returns.

  4. F

    Fragility

    The denominator. What single failure would end it: a customer, a contract, a platform, a person? The smaller F, the stronger the case.

Four fields.

No two snowflakes share a structure, and no two theses do. Yet every crystal obeys the same six-fold symmetry. Every investment we make answers to the same four variables.

Technology

Software and hardware that other systems depend on. The layers beneath the interface, where switching costs are real and engineering quality is measurable.

Trust, engineered.

Cyber, physical and identity security. The systems that let institutions, companies and people operate with confidence in an adversarial world.

We look for
Mission-critical deployments; demand driven by compliance and consequence; proven resilience.

Products sold on fear cycles; security that cannot be verified.

Multi-year contracts already won; deep domain expertise; a record of delivery.

Single-contract concentration; dependence on one political cycle.

Ego

A remarkable GPU,
left in the dark.

Apple silicon carries some of the finest graphics processors ever put inside a computer. Yet Windows and Linux running in a virtual machine on a Mac could not use them, and drew every frame on the processor instead. That is not right, least of all in the age of artificial intelligence, when the GPU is where the work is done.

Ego VM is a hypervisor for Apple silicon with real GPU acceleration. Windows and Linux run on the Mac with its own graphics chip: games, professional software and private AI models, sealed inside a machine that never leaves your desk.

Compute is becoming the raw material of this century. The most capable hardware many people already own should be entirely theirs to use.

VP.NET

Trust is fleeting.
Mathematics is not.

Virtual private networks have existed for decades, and nearly all of them rest on a single request: trust us. The provider can see every site you visit and promises not to look. But promises are rewritten, companies are sold and servers are seized.

VP.NET is verifiable privacy. Traffic is handled inside sealed Intel SGX enclaves that even its own engineers cannot open, and anyone can confirm, cryptographically, exactly which code is running there. Spying is not forbidden by policy. It is impossible by design.

We are part of VP.NET because we believe in security through mathematics and verification, not through trust. Trust is not a constant. A proof is.

bmail

Correspondence deserves
a proof, not a promise.

Email is half a century old, and most of it still rests on a server its provider can read. The largest mailboxes in the world are scanned, indexed and surrendered on request. Private alternatives have long asked ordinary people to manage keys, or simply to trust whoever runs the machine.

bmail, from bimil, the Korean word for secret, is verifiably private email. Mail, calendar, contacts and drive are encrypted on your own device with post-quantum cryptography, sealed in hardware enclaves and bound to public key transparency. Not even bmail can read them, and anyone can check the proofs.

It is the same conviction, applied to the most personal record a person keeps. A letter should be private because it can be shown to be, not because someone says so.

Dissent

An internet a censor
cannot afford to block.

Every circumvention tool until now has run on servers a censor can find and ban. The censor only needs to win once per server, and in time it always does. Hundreds of millions of people live behind such walls.

Dissent is a VPN that lives only behind Cloudflare, among the fifth of the web that Cloudflare fronts. Each connection is ordinary HTTPS with Encrypted Client Hello, indistinguishable from a visit to millions of legitimate sites. To block Dissent, a state must block a fifth of the internet for its own people.

Freedom that depends on going unnoticed is fragile. Freedom that costs too much to take away is durable. We back the second kind.

ChimeTV

Representation is
not a niche.

More than twenty million Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders live in the United States. For most of television’s history, they rarely saw themselves on it, in front of the camera or behind it.

ChimeTV is the only 24/7 English-language cable network for Asian American and Pacific Islander stories. Drama, comedy, food, travel, sport and news, with more than seventy percent of it new to American television.

We believe North American television should look like North America. Audiences who have waited a long time to be seen are among the most loyal in media, and the network built for them will last.

Five stages.
One direction.

  1. Observation

    We read widely and record everything. Most of what we see, we quietly decline.

  2. Hypothesis

    We write the thesis down, including the precise conditions under which it would be wrong.

  3. Measurement

    Unit economics, technical architecture, regulatory surface, and the people. Evidence before enthusiasm.

  4. Conviction

    We commit only when the thesis survives our best attempts to break it. Then we commit fully.

  5. Stewardship

    We hold patiently and stay close. Available when needed, invisible when not.
